Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2025

Daily Funds is a simple budgeting app designed to help you track your money across budgets, accounts, and categories.

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers by default. All data (budgets, accounts, categories, transactions, statistics) remain only on your device.

Cloud backup or sync (if enabled)

In future versions, optional cloud backup or synchronization may be offered (for example, through iCloud or another provider). If you choose to enable such a feature, your data may be stored and processed by that service according to their own privacy policy. You will always be able to turn sync off, and this page will be updated with specific details before such features are released.

Permissions

  • Notifications: Used only for budget reminders or monthly summaries. You can disable notifications at any time in the system settings of your device.
  • File access / storage (optional): If you export backups or reports, the app writes files to your chosen location. These files stay under your control.

Third-party services

Daily Funds does not use in-app analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools that follow you across other apps or websites.

Children’s privacy

Daily Funds is not specifically directed at children. It is a utility app for general use by adults and families.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
